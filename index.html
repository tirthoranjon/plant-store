Plant Care Guides

Proper plant care requires balancing water, light, humidity, and nutrients to mimic a plant's natural environment.Because different plants have unique needs, understanding these fundamental categories will keep your indoor and outdoor garden thriving.

  1. The Knuckle Test: Never water on a strict calendar schedule.Stick your finger 5 centimeters into the soil; if it feels damp, do not water. If it feels completely dry, water the plant thoroughly until water leaks out of the bottom drainage holes.
  2. Drainage is Mandatory: Never buy a pot without baseline drainage holes. Trapped standing water at the bottom of a pot cuts off oxygen to the roots, causing deadly root rot
  3. Match Plant to Light: Look at your room's natural windows before buying.High-light plants (like succulents) will wither in dark corners, while low-light plants (like snake plants) will scorch under intense, direct sun rays.
